About Us

Harbor Light Community Center has been a cornerstone of our neighborhood since 1985. We are dedicated to fostering a vibrant, inclusive, and supportive community environment where every individual can thrive.

Our programs span education, health and wellness, the arts, and civic engagement — all designed with the community's needs at the heart of everything we do.

Our Services

We offer a wide range of programs and services designed to support every member of our community:

  • 🎓 After-School Tutoring & Mentorship
  • 🏋️ Fitness & Wellness Classes
  • 🎨 Arts, Crafts & Creative Workshops
  • 👴 Senior Citizens' Day Program
  • 💼 Job Readiness & Career Coaching
  • 🌿 Community Garden & Green Spaces
