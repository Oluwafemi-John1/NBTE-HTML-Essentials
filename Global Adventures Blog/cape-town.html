🌊 Cape Town, South Africa

Cape Town is one of those rare cities that looks exactly like its photographs — and somehow still manages to exceed every expectation. Tucked between mountains and two oceans, it is dramatic, diverse, warm-hearted, and achingly beautiful. I arrived a visitor and left feeling like I'd come home. 🏔️

🏔️ Iconic Landmarks

The Table Mountain National Park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the city's most iconic landmark — hike it for an unforgettable challenge, or take the cable car for equally jaw-dropping views. Boulders Beach in Simon's Town is home to a thriving colony of African penguins, waddling about as if the beach belongs to them (it does). Cape Point, at the southern tip of the peninsula, where the Atlantic crashes against ancient cliffs, is one of the most dramatic natural spectacles on the planet.

🍷 Culinary Delights

Cape Town's food culture is as diverse as its people. The V&A Waterfront market overflows with freshly caught Cape crayfish, artisan cheeses, and Cape Malay curries that carry the warmth of the city's layered history. A day trip to the Stellenbosch Wine Route is non-negotiable — the Chenin Blancs and Pinotages grown on these ancient slopes are world class.

  • 🦞 Cape Crayfish (Kreef) – Grilled simply with lemon and butter. Perfection.
  • 🍛 Cape Malay Curry – Fragrant, mildly spiced, deeply comforting.
  • 🥩 Braai – South Africa's beloved BBQ tradition, more ritual than meal.
  • 🍷 Stellenbosch Wine – Award-winning wines 45 minutes from the city.

🎵 Cultural Experiences

Bo-Kaap, the iconic neighbourhood of brightly painted houses on the slopes of Signal Hill, is the heart of Cape Malay culture and one of the most photographed streets in Africa. The Zeitz Museum of Contemporary Art Africa (MOCAA) is a world-class institution celebrating African creative voices, housed in a breathtaking converted grain silo. On Saturday mornings, the Oranjezicht City Farm Market in De Waterkant buzzes with musicians, chefs, and Capetonians living their very best lives.

💭 Personal Reflections

We watched the sunrise from the summit of Lion's Head on our final morning — a tough 90-minute hike in the dark rewarded by one of the most spectacular views I have ever witnessed. The Atlantic to the west, the Indian Ocean to the east, the city still sleeping below us, and the sky on fire. My friend turned to me and simply said, "We have to come back." No argument from me.

"Cape Town doesn't let you go. It plants a flag in your chest and stays there." 🌍
